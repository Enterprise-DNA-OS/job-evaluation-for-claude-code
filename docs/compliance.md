# Evidence checks for job evaluation

Checked 26 October 2026. The compliance command checks the presence and currency of evaluation evidence. It does not decide legal compliance, certify a job-evaluation scheme, establish equal value or calculate equal pay. The supplied scoring model is illustrative; a competent evaluator must validate a business method.

## Sources

- NZ-EJE: [Employment New Zealand, Evaluation of equitable jobs](https://www.employment.govt.nz/fair-work-practices/pay-and-gender-equity/evaluation-of-equitable-jobs). Job evaluation can contain unintentional gender bias; collecting complete job information and examining the factors helps make the basis visible.
- AU-EQUITY: [Fair Work Ombudsman, Gender pay equity best practice guide](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/tools-and-resources/best-practice-guides/gender-pay-equity). The guide addresses non-discriminatory assessment of job demands and work of equal or comparable value. It is context for review, not a formula implemented here.
- HOUSE: the organisation's documented evaluation controls, set out in [methodology](methodology.md). These support the evidence approach above but are not requirements stated by those authorities.

## Checks and their limits

| Check | Recorded evidence | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| PROFILE | Job purpose and duties exist | NZ-EJE; content still needs human validation |
| NO_EVALUATION | A local evaluation exists | NZ-EJE evidence support; import alone is not evaluation |
| MISSING_FACTOR | Each of the four factors has a level, rationale and evidence | NZ-EJE and AU-EQUITY context; four-factor implementation is a house design |
| OWNER | A person is responsible for review | HOUSE, not a statutory appointment |
| REVIEW_DUE | The organisation's chosen review date has not passed | HOUSE; no legal review interval is claimed |
| STALE_BASIS | Evaluation uses the current job revision | HOUSE; any field change requires review |
| PANEL_REVIEW | An independent named decision is recorded | HOUSE; names are operator labels, not identity verification |
| REJECTED | The latest evaluation needs replacement | HOUSE |
| OPEN_APPEAL | A challenge remains unresolved | HOUSE; this is not a statutory grievance process |
| APPEAL_OVERDUE | The attention view compares the organisation's due date with today | HOUSE; not a legal deadline |

The CLI also blocks approval unless a reviewer other than the author records a decision rationale and bias-review evidence. It does not assess the truth or sufficiency of that evidence. No wages, salary surveys, protected-characteristic analysis, pay-gap calculation, legal filing or payroll decision is made. Obtain the appropriate specialist advice before changing employment terms.
